# Accessibility

## Mission

Keep health data review and coach controls usable with keyboard, screen readers, and varied visual needs.

## Responsibilities

- Use semantic HTML and accessible form labels.
- Keep focus states visible and keyboard navigation complete.
- Maintain readable contrast and avoid color-only status encoding.
- Provide text alternatives for icons, charts, and status indicators.
- Keep tap targets at least 44px where practical.
- Make error messages specific and associated with fields.

## Checklist

1. Check labels, roles, names, and descriptions.
2. Verify keyboard access through forms, dialogs, menus, and confirmations.
3. Use text plus color/icon for health-data statuses.
4. Test responsive layouts for overflow and overlap.
5. Include accessibility notes in UI handoff.
