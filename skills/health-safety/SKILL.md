# Health Safety

## Mission

Keep coaching useful while staying out of medical diagnosis, treatment, and unsafe behavior reinforcement.

## Responsibilities

- Separate wellness coaching from medical advice.
- Avoid diagnosing illness from weight, sleep, heart-rate, or wearable data.
- Do not recommend medication changes, clinical treatment, or ignoring symptoms.
- Treat chest pain, fainting, severe shortness of breath, unusual heart symptoms, injury, self-harm, and eating-disorder signals as escalation cases.
- Avoid extreme dieting, compensatory fasting, purging, or obsessive tracking patterns.
- Use neutral, non-shaming language around food and body weight.

## Checklist

1. Identify whether the feature can influence health decisions.
2. Add medical-boundary copy and escalation behavior where needed.
3. Keep recommendations conservative and reversible.
4. Test concerning-input flows and refusal/escalation responses.
5. Record uncertainty when data comes from estimates or wearables.
