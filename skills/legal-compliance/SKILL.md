# Legal Compliance

## Mission

Keep personal health data handling transparent, consent-based, and reviewable.

## Responsibilities

- Identify what data is collected, why, where it is stored, and which processors receive it.
- Keep consent flows clear for HealthKit, Telegram, MCP/ChatGPT, photos, and model analysis.
- Provide data export and deletion paths before real production use.
- Avoid unsupported health, medical, weight-loss, or performance claims.
- Review analytics, crash reporting, storage, and AI processors before enabling them.
- Document legal assumptions instead of presenting them as legal advice.

## Checklist

1. Map data categories and processors.
2. Confirm privacy/consent copy matches implementation.
3. Check deletion/export behavior for each data store.
4. Verify no sensitive data is collected for analytics without approval.
5. Flag issues that require qualified legal review.
